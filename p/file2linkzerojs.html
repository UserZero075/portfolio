File2LinkZeroJS

Telegram file-to-link bot powered by ZerogramJS (MTProto) and JsonFlux. Forward any file, get download/watch/share links. Single-page WebApp with admin panel, moderation, i18n, quotas and live updates via Socket.IO. No 50MB limit, no external database.
